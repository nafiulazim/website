Gender-based violence: partners or relatives killed 47,000 women and girls in 2025. That is 129 every day.

For the 16 Days of Activism (25 November to 10 December): how many women and girls are killed by people close to them, where the risk is highest, how common partner and sexual violence is, and how rarely survivors reach the police.

NABy Nafiul AzimInternational development professional
Data compiled 27 Nov 2026Gender-based violence⚥ Gender & InclusionGlobal
Share
Key takeaways
  1. About 47,000 women and girls worldwide were killed on purpose by an intimate partner or family member in 2025. That is 60% of the 78,000 women and girls killed intentionally that year, or 129 a day (UNODC and UN Women).
  2. In 2025, Africa had the highest rate of women and girls killed by a partner or relative: 2.9 per 100,000 women and girls, against 0.5 in Europe. In Europe, this rate fell 28% from 2010 to 2025, while all homicides fell 43%.
  3. WHO estimates that about 840 million women, nearly 1 in 3, have experienced partner or sexual violence in their lifetime (data from 2000 to 2023). In a separate UN Women study of 54 countries, only 7% of women who experienced violence reported it to the police.
