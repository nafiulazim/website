Rich countries promised $100 billion. The new goal is $300 billion.

Ahead of COP31 in Antalya, here is what developed countries have delivered, what the next goals ask for, and who is still waiting for the money.

NABy Nafiul AzimInternational development professional
Data compiled 4 Nov 2026Climate finance🌿 Climate & EnvironmentGlobal
Share
Key takeaways
  1. Developed countries delivered US$136.7 billion in 2024. The new goal is US$300 billion a year by 2035, about 2.2× more.
  2. Low-income countries received just 7% of 2024 climate finance.
  3. Developing countries need US$310–365 billion a year for adaptation; international public adaptation finance was US$26 billion in 2023.

The bigger picture
$6.3T a year
CPI 2025

Climate Policy Initiative estimates the world needs $6.3 trillion a year in climate finance for 2024–2030. Tracked flows reached $1.9 trillion in 2023, and early data show they passed $2 trillion in 2024. Most of it is raised and spent inside countries.
