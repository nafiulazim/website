What the gap looks like on the ground

  • Afghanistan: about one third of the $1.7B plan funded, leaving a $1.1B gap.OCHA Afghanistan, 2026
  • Gaza: fewer than 1 million of 2.1 million people get monthly food aid from October.OCHA oPt, 22 Oct 2026
  • DRC: 2026 requirements cut 45%; monthly food targets cut up to 73%.OCHA DRC, Jan 2026
  • Sudan: only 7.5% of 2.2 million people targeted have received shelter aid.GHO 2026, Sudan

Put the number in perspective

The UN says the full appeal could be funded if everyone earning over $100,000 a year gave $0.20 a day. The $23B priority ask is about 1% of the $2.7 trillion the world spent on defence last year.

For live figures, check OCHA Financial Tracking Service and humanitarianaction.info. Figures here were compiled on 28 Oct 2026.
